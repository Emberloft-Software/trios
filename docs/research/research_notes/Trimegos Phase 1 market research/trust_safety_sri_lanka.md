# Trust and safety risk fact base: Sri Lanka (for Trimegos, feeds 06-trust-safety.md)

Research date: 2026-10-31. All URLs accessed 2026-10-31 unless noted. Most facts came from search-result summaries. The ICNL Civic Freedom Monitor and the Newswire 25 Oct 2026 article were the only pages fetched in full. Before quoting any number publicly, re-open the primary source. **This is not legal advice.** Items marked [LAWYER] need a Sri Lankan lawyer to confirm.

## 1. Harassment: public spaces, transport, online, image-based abuse

### Takeaway
Sexual harassment of women in public transport is close to universal (UNFPA 2017: 90%) and almost never reported to police (4%). The first national TFGBV study (UN Women/UNFPA, 2025) finds 57% feel unsafe online and nearly 1 in 3 have experienced technology-facilitated GBV. A meetup app's main risks are therefore harassment before the meet (chat/DMs), on the way there, and image-based abuse afterwards. These risks matter more than violence at the venue itself.

### Cited Findings
- UNFPA-commissioned national study: 90% of women and girls had been sexually harassed at least once on public buses and trains. Sample: 2,500 women and girls aged 15–35 from all districts of the nine provinces (commissioned 2015, released March 2017). [UNFPA Asia-Pacific](https://aa.unfpa.org/news/ninety-cent-sri-lankan-women-endure-sexual-harassment-public-transport-unfpa-study-shows); [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/Special-Report/unfpa-survey-90-of-women-have-experienced-sexual-harassment-on-public-transport-in-sri-lanka/22-600298); [UNFPA Sri Lanka publication page](https://srilanka.unfpa.org/publications/sexual-harassment-public-buses-and-trains)
- Same study: 97% said perpetrators were male. About 25% said harassment happened monthly and about 12% daily. Only 4% sought help from police. 44% said it affected their personal lives, 37% their work performance and 29% their school performance. — [UNFPA Asia-Pacific](https://aa.unfpa.org/news/ninety-cent-sri-lankan-women-endure-sexual-harassment-public-transport-unfpa-study-shows)
- Note: some UNFPA pages say "women" and others "women and girls" (the sample includes 15–17 year olds). — [UNFPA Asia-Pacific](https://aa.unfpa.org/news/ninety-cent-sri-lankan-women-endure-sexual-harassment-public-transport-unfpa-study-shows)
- In 2024, UN Women with UNFPA commissioned Sri Lanka's first comprehensive study on technology-facilitated gender-based violence (TFGBV). The 102-page report was published 22 Aug 2025. 57% of respondents felt unsafe online, and nearly one in three had personally experienced some form of TFGBV. — [UN Women press release, Nov 2025](https://asiapacific.unwomen.org/en/stories/press-release/2025/11/online-abuse-silences-women-and-marginalized-groups-in-sri-lanka); [UN Women publication page](https://asiapacific.unwomen.org/en/digital-library/publications/2025/09/research-study-on-technology-facilitated-violence-experienced-by-women); [UNFPA Sri Lanka](https://srilanka.unfpa.org/en/publications/research-study-technology-facilitated-violence-experienced-women-and-marginalized)
- Hashtag Generation, Ghosha and the Centre for Policy Alternatives studied Facebook pages (2018–2019). They found sexual harassment, non-consensual sharing of intimate images, and normalised sexist commentary that escalated to violence against women and LBT people online and offline. — [Groundviews 2018](https://groundviews.org/2018/01/15/technology-related-violence-against-women-and-girls-in-sri-lanka-key-trends); [Groundviews 2019](https://groundviews.org/2019/06/27/opinions-btch-technology-based-violence-against-women-in-sri-lanka)
- Hashtag Generation runs a hotline for survivors of online violence. A May 2026 Groundviews piece says Delete Nothing and Hashtag Generation set up the "Prathya" hotline for reporting image-based sexual abuse, now run by Hashtag Generation. It also says Sri Lankan law lacks specific provisions on consent-based image sharing. — [Groundviews, 17 May 2026](https://groundviews.org/2026/05/17/the-need-to-address-image-based-sexual-abuse-in-a-digital-sri-lanka/)
- Sri Lanka CERT data for Jan–Jul 2025 lists sexual harassment among social-media complaint categories, alongside fake Facebook accounts, defamation and WhatsApp hacking (6,512 social-media complaints in total). — [News 1st, 22 Sep 2025](https://www.newsfirst.lk/2025/09/22/over-6-500-cybercrime-complaints-reported-in-first-seven-months-of-the-year)

### Inferences
- Getting to and from a gig by bus or train is itself a harassment risk for women. Safety copy should cover the journey (share trip, prefer daylight or ride-hailing late at night), not just the venue.
- Women rarely report to police (4%), so in-app reports will be the main signal Trimegos receives. Expect under-reporting even in-app. Keep reporting low-friction and allow anonymous reports.
- Image-based abuse is a documented local harm, and photos unlock after a gig fills. Watch for screenshots of unlocked profile photos being misused. Consider a reporting path for "my photo was shared off-platform" that points to Hashtag Generation/Prathya and CERT.

### Gaps
- No newer national prevalence figure for street/transport harassment after 2017 was found.
- Women In Need annual case statistics were not found.
- The full text of the 2025 TFGBV study was not read, so no breakdown by type (e.g. image-based abuse share) is available.

## 2. Scams: romance, job, investment, OTP/WhatsApp hijacking

### Takeaway
Cyber complaints are rising fast. Sri Lanka CERT received more than 12,650 complaints in 2025, mostly fake or hacked social accounts and financial scams. WhatsApp OTP hijacking is the dominant warning theme. On 25 Oct 2026 police specifically warned about fake "dating apps" that are malicious APKs stealing banking credentials. That warning makes "a new app where you meet strangers" a direct trust hurdle for Trimegos.

### Cited Findings
- Sri Lanka CERT received more than 12,650 complaints about social media misuse and cyber incidents in 2025. Fake and hacked accounts and financial scams on social platforms make up a large share. — [Ada Derana](https://adaderana.lk/news/116652/sri-lanka-cert-warns-of-growing-cyber-threats-as-complaints-exceed-12600-in-2025); [NAMPA](https://www.nampa.org/text/22821495)
- CERT: 6,512 social-media-linked complaints in the first seven months of 2025. — [News 1st](https://www.newsfirst.lk/2025/09/22/over-6-500-cybercrime-complaints-reported-in-first-seven-months-of-the-year)
- A separate measure: over 5,400 cybercrime cases in 2025 per the CID, which warned of job and investment scams. CID counts and CERT counts are different measures. — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/front-page/Over-5-400-cybercrime-cases-in-2025-CID-warns-of-job-investment-scams-Cybercrime-rises-in-Sri-Lanka/238-315197)
- Daily Mirror reported that online scams doubled in a year, with police increasing crackdowns (date not confirmed). — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Online-scams-doubles-in-a-year-police-increase-crackdowns/108-330212)
- July 2025: the CID warned of fake calls and messages stealing WhatsApp OTPs and said OTPs should never be shared. — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/CID-warns-public-of-WhatsApp-OTP-scams/108-315511); [News 1st, 30 Jul 2025](https://english.newsfirst.lk/2025/07/30/don%e2%80%99t-share-your-otp-whatsapp-scams-are-on-the-rise)
- CID reported a surge in complaints about money being solicited via hijacked WhatsApp accounts. Account holders often notice only days later, after contacts have been defrauded. — [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news.php?nid=114269)
- SLCERT: once an OTP is shared, the original user loses the WhatsApp account and the attacker messages their contacts asking for money. — [Daily Mirror, 28 Oct 2024](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/breaking-news/SLCERT-warns-WhatsApp-users-against-sharing-OTPs-to-prevent-hacking/108-294712); [Sunday Observer, 1 Dec 2024](https://sundayobserver.lk/2024/12/01/news-features/39112/alarming-trend-in-whatsapp-account-hacks-in-sri-lanka)
- Police warned of an APK-file scam sent over WhatsApp/Telegram that targets bank accounts. — [Ada Derana Biz](https://bizenglish.adaderana.lk/police-warn-of-new-digital-scam-targeting-bank-accounts-via-apk-files/)
- **25 Oct 2026, Sri Lanka Police:** scammers build friendships or romances on dating apps, Facebook, WhatsApp and Telegram, then persuade victims to install a "specialised" app. These "may appear to be dating apps" but can be malicious APKs that capture online banking credentials. Advice: be cautious with strangers online, don't install apps from unknown sources, don't share OTPs or passwords, and don't share personal photos or videos with unknown people. No statistics given ("several incidents"). — [Newswire, 25 Oct 2026](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/10/25/police-warn-public-of-scams-involving-fake-dating-apps/)
- Sri Lanka CERT (May 2026) warned of fake traffic-fine SMS/WhatsApp messages that harvest card details, OTPs and NIC data. — [Ada Derana](https://adaderana.lk/news/cmqql7yb10003356pgrwf8bfm) (title not fully confirmed from snippet)
- Earlier CERT data: about 80% of Facebook-related complaints concerned fake accounts (older article, likely 2018). — [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/180107/news/helpless-users-pile-on-complaints-against-facebook-fakes-276315.html)

### Inferences
- Trimegos is a PWA installed from the browser, not from Play Store. Given the police warning about fake dating-app APKs, users may see "install this app" as a scam signal. Trust copy should say plainly: "never an APK, never asks for OTPs or bank details". Trimegos should never ask users to sideload anything.
- WhatsApp hijacking means a crew member's WhatsApp identity could be compromised. Keeping coordination inside the app's own chat (not pushing groups to WhatsApp) reduces exposure. Chat warnings for phone numbers and links are already in place and fit this threat.
- Romance/investment pivots ("let's talk on Telegram", "I can get you a job abroad") are plausible abuse patterns inside gig chat. Consider adding scam terms (crypto, investment, job abroad, OTP, Telegram) to `moderation_terms`.

### Gaps
- No official full-year 2024 CERT total was found. No published breakdown of romance-scam losses in LKR was found.
- The CID cyber crime unit's reporting channel and number were not confirmed.
- The Sri Lanka CERT hotline: the brief says "101". This was **not verified** in this research. Confirm on cert.gov.lk before publishing.

## 3. Dating-app perception and incidents

### Takeaway
No reliable data was found on Tinder/Bumble usage or on public attitudes to dating apps in Sri Lanka. Media coverage is dominated by scams, sextortion, the online sex trade, and one reported Grindr extortion ring. "Not a dating app" positioning is defensible and necessary. The safety story must also counter the "meeting strangers from an app = scam/sex/blackmail" frame.

### Cited Findings
- Five youths aged 17–20 were arrested over an extortion racket in which one suspect lured the victim on Grindr by offering a "body massage". Date not visible in the result. — [Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/rznm)
- Sextortion: Sunday Times (Dec 2019) said Sri Lanka had no specific law targeting sextortion. A 2021 academic paper found existing Penal Code provisions could be used. — [Sunday Times, 22 Dec 2019](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/191222/sunday-times-2/sextortion-a-new-name-an-age-old-crime-383934.html); [SLIIT repository](https://rda.sliit.lk/entities/publication/0b1ede76-5f6b-459a-8528-f19e3975865a)
- "Double victimisation" (stigma, family exposure) discourages sextortion victims from seeking legal help. — [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/sri-lankan-sextortion-technologys-dark-and-dirty-power-play/)
- Police say the sex trade has moved from spas to encrypted messaging apps, and fear of stigma means few formal complaints. — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Online-sex-trade-intensifies-in-Sri-Lanka/108-319434); [Tamil Guardian](https://www.tamilguardian.com/node/953681)
- Sri Lankan graduates trafficked to East Asian scam compounds were made to run romance scams against Westerners on Hinge, Boo, Tinder and POF. — [Daily Mirror](https://dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Sri-Lankan-graduates-scamming-Westerners-via-dating-apps/108-253755)
- Police warning on fake dating apps (APK malware), 25 Oct 2026. See section 2. — [Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/10/25/police-warn-public-of-scams-involving-fake-dating-apps/)
- Cyber crimes targeting children are rising, and authorities are running awareness efforts (Sept 2024). — [Sunday Times, 1 Sep 2024](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/240901/news/as-cyber-crimes-targeting-children-rise-authorities-seek-to-raise-awareness-569961.html)

### Inferences
- The local media frame for "apps to meet strangers" is overwhelmingly negative (scam, sextortion, sex trade). Users, and especially parents, will come in with that frame. Trimegos copy should name what it is not (no 1:1 meets, no private photos, no image sharing in chat, no APK, never asks for money).
- The Grindr case shows the pattern in which a lure through an app leads to a private, secluded meeting and then robbery or extortion. Trimegos's rules of minimum 3 people and public places directly break this pattern and should be presented as the core safety design. Watch for gigs that try to move to private venues ("my place", "room").

### Gaps
- No usage data for Tinder, Bumble or Bumble BFF in Sri Lanka (downloads, MAU) was found. App-intelligence sources (Sensor Tower/data.ai) would be needed.
- No public-opinion survey on dating apps in Sri Lanka was found.
- Few Sri Lankan robbery or blackmail cases tied to straight dating apps (vs. Grindr) were found. Searches mostly returned Indian and Malaysian cases.

## 4. Family and cultural norms around mixed-gender socialising

### Takeaway
Most unmarried youth do have romantic relationships, and disclosure to parents is rising. Parental approval still matters a lot, and parents are more protective outside Colombo. No survey broken down by community (Sinhala/Tamil/Muslim) on mixed-gender friendship was found. This is a key gap for user research.

### Cited Findings
- 2019 field survey of 1,057 unmarried 15–24 year olds in three districts (not nationally representative; drawn from vocational centres, tuition classes and NGOs): 89% had had one or more romantic relationships. Over 81% of males and 85% of females in current relationships went out with their partner. — [Sri Lanka Journal of Social Sciences](https://sljss.sljol.info/en/articles/10.4038/sljss.v45i1.8106)
- 1986 Family Planning Association survey of 2,466 unmarried 16–24 year olds: about a third favoured love marriage, a third preferred arranged marriage, and a third had no fixed view (cited as still the most comprehensive such study). — [Sri Lanka Journal of Social Sciences](https://sljss.sljol.info/en/articles/10.4038/sljss.v45i1.8106)
- University of Peradeniya study (2011, single campus): over 75% of students in heterosexual relationships had introduced the partner to parents, vs 25% in an earlier Peradeniya study. Female students were more likely to disclose than males. Same-sex relationships were not disclosed to families. — [University of Peradeniya repository](https://ir.lib.pdn.ac.lk/items/1ce2e7b5-e0a3-4524-8e38-8aaeb2b26532)
- A Colombo psychologist says young people still find parental approval "very, very important". Journalistic accounts describe parents in rural areas as more protective than in Colombo. — [Life.lk](https://www.life.lk/article/interviews/dating-and-youth/52/12422); [The Juggernaut](https://www.thejuggernaut.com/dating-in-sri-lanka)

### Inferences
- Activity-first, group, public, daytime gigs are the format most likely to pass a parent's approval. Women-only gigs are likely to be important for adoption among more conservative families. This is untested and worth validating in interviews by community.
- Colombo-first launch fits where norms are most permissive.

### Gaps
- No survey by ethnic/religious community (Sinhala, Tamil, Muslim) on mixed-gender socialising or women's freedom of movement was found. Searches for Asia Foundation and CENWOR data were not completed due to tool-call limits. Recommended follow-up: Asia Foundation surveys, CENWOR, the Department of Census DHS (women's decision-making/mobility module), and the British Council/UNDP youth surveys.

## 5. LGBTQ: legal status and entrapment risk

### Takeaway
As of the latest sources found (Oct 2025), Penal Code s.365 and s.365A still criminalise consensual same-sex conduct. A 2023 Supreme Court determination found Premnath Dolawatte's decriminalisation bill constitutional (simple majority enough), but the bill was not adopted by the 2020–2024 parliament. In Oct 2025 the justice minister said no amendment had been moved. No 2026 change was found. [LAWYER] to confirm status as of Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- May 2023: the Supreme Court ruled the proposed amendment to decriminalise consensual same-sex relations constitutional, so it could pass with a simple majority. — [Outright International](https://outrightinternational.org/our-work/asia/sri-lanka); [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/PC3XlOY2MmrT7IBgEaFh)
- The bill (Private Member's Bill by Premnath C. Dolawatte) would amend s.365 to cover only bestiality and repeal s.365A entirely. — [Wikipedia, Article 365](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_365_of_the_Sri_Lankan_Penal_Code); [Human Dignity Trust](https://www.humandignitytrust.org/country-profile/sri-lanka/)
- The bill was not adopted by the 2020–2024 parliament. In October 2025 the justice minister said the government had not moved to amend ss.365/365A and called for public discussion first. — [Outright International](https://outrightinternational.org/our-work/asia/sri-lanka)
- Conflict: a fragmentary passage in the Human Dignity Trust profile says the sections were "removed in August" with no year given. This was not corroborated anywhere and is inconsistent with Outright's Oct 2025 report. Treat as unverified. — [Human Dignity Trust](https://www.humandignitytrust.org/country-profile/sri-lanka/)
- 2022: then Foreign Minister Ali Sabry said the government would decriminalise but not legalise same-sex marriage. — [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/DTlRaTHSwT1O8hESDdqF)
- CEDAW, *Rosanna Flamer-Caldera v Sri Lanka* (Comm. 134/2018), adopted 21 Feb 2022 and published 23 Mar 2022. The Committee found that criminalising same-sex intimacy (s.365A) violated her rights. It also found the law deterred her from reporting abuse for fear of arrest, and urged decriminalisation. The decision is not binding on domestic law. — [Human Dignity Trust case page](https://www.humandignitytrust.org/what-we-do/cases/sri-lanka-case-before-un-committee-on-the-elimination-of-discrimination-against-women-cedaw/); [Oxford Human Rights Hub](https://ohrh.law.ox.ac.uk/rosanna-flamer-caldera-v-sri-lanka-cedaw-2022-the-first-international-case-on-lesbian-criminalisation/); [ICJ](https://www.icj.org/icj-joint-statement-on-same-sex-relationships-in-sri-lanka)
- ICJ and others urge repeal of Penal Code ss.365, 365A and 399 (cheating by personation, which is used against trans people). — [ICJ](https://www.icj.org/icj-joint-statement-on-same-sex-relationships-in-sri-lanka)
- The ICCPR Act No. 56 of 2007 "has been used against critics of the government" (general expression context, not LGBTQ-specific). — [ICNL](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/sri-lanka)
- App-based entrapment pattern: Grindr lure, then extortion of the victim (see section 3). — [Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/rznm)

### Inferences
- LGBTQ users face double exposure. They may be extorted, and they may fear reporting because the conduct is criminalised (the CEDAW finding). Trimegos should not collect sexual orientation, and should never expose anything that implies it. Moderation must treat outing threats and blackmail as top-severity.
- Trimegos is not a dating app and is group-based, which lowers entrapment risk compared with Grindr. Gigs explicitly themed as LGBTQ meetups could still be targeted by people trying to identify attendees. Policy decisions here need input from Equal Ground and a lawyer.

### Gaps
- Vagrants Ordinance (1841) use against LGBTQ/trans people: no source retrieved.
- Equal Ground's safety guidance for app users: not retrieved.
- Status of the decriminalisation bill in 2026: no 2026 source found. [LAWYER]

## 6. Online Safety Act No. 9 of 2024 and related law

### Takeaway
The OSA (Jan 2024) created a five-member Online Safety Commission that can issue notices or directives to internet intermediaries, with courts able to act on "declared online locations". On 26 Jun 2026 the President said the OSA would not be implemented in its current form and would be replaced, with Cabinet approval. As of Sep 2026, ICNL says the original Act **remains in force** and replacement legislation had not been tabled or enacted. Sources conflict on whether the 2024 amendment was enacted. [LAWYER] for how a small PWA counts as an "internet intermediary" and the current takedown deadlines.

### Cited Findings
- The OSA was enacted January 2024 over civil-society opposition and establishes an Online Safety Commission "with extensive powers relating to online content". Reuters (Jan 2024) reported it would make platforms liable for content a five-member commission deems illegal. — [ICNL Civic Freedom Monitor](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/sri-lanka) (fetched)
- Act text: [Parliament, Online Safety Act No. 9 of 2024](https://www.parliament.lk/uploads/acts/gbills/english/6311.pdf)
- The Act imposes no standing registration or product-design duty on intermediaries. Obligations arise when the Commission (or, for declared online locations, a Magistrate's Court) issues a directive. — [Magist.io summary, 2026](https://magist.io/regulations/sri-lanka-online-safety) (commercial summary, lower confidence)
- 2024 amendment bill: [Online Safety (Amendment) Bill, documents.gov.lk 535-2024](https://www.documents.gov.lk/view/bills/2024/7/535-2024_E.pdf). Freedom House (FOTN 2025) says parliament approved amendments in August 2024 that "embedded intermediary liability safeguards" and broadened some criminal provisions. — [Freedom House FOTN 2025](https://freedomhouse.org/country/sri-lanka/freedom-net/2025). **Conflict:** ICNL (Sept 2026) says "the proposed amendments had not been enacted or tested in practice". — [ICNL](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/sri-lanka). [LAWYER]
- Feb 2025: Cabinet endorsed an OSA review. Aug 2025: limited public consultation. May 2025: Cabinet appointed a committee to work on amendments. A private member's repeal bill presented in May 2025 was pending its second reading. — [ICNL](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/sri-lanka); [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/News/Cabinet-nod-to-amend-Online-Safety-Act/56-758443); [Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/country/sri-lanka/freedom-net/2025)
- 26 Jun 2026: President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said the OSA would not be implemented in its current form and would be replaced by new legislation (Cabinet approval obtained). — [Lanka Newspapers, 26 Jun 2026](https://www.lankanewspapers.com/2026/06/26/president-dissanayake-confirms-online-safety-act-to-be-scrapped-and-replaced-with-new-legislation); [ICNL](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/sri-lanka)
- As of September 2026, "the original Act remains in force" and replacement legislation had not been tabled. — [ICNL](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/sri-lanka)
- A Daily FT item dated 16 Sep 2026 in search results says OSA provisions "are being implemented at present". This may be an older article with a misleading dateline and conflicts with the June 2026 announcement. — [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/News/Cabinet-nod-to-amend-Online-Safety-Act/56-758443)
- Related expression laws: the ICCPR Act No. 56 of 2007 and the PTA are also used against speech. In Sept 2026 the Protection of the State from Terrorism Bill was gazetted to replace the PTA (not enacted as of Oct 2026). — [ICNL](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/sri-lanka)
- **PDPA No. 9 of 2022:** amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act No. 22 of 2025 (third reading 21 Oct 2025; Speaker certified 30 Oct 2025). The amendment revises s.17 (refusal of access, rectification and erasure) and s.24 (DPO), among others. Gazette No. 2498/16 sets **1 January 2027** as the commencement date for sections 2–3, Part I and Part III. The original 18 Mar 2025 enforcement date was postponed. — [DPA: Amendment Act No. 22 of 2025](https://dpa.gov.lk/acts/22-2025_E_251104_201549.pdf); [DPA Circular 01/2026](https://dpa.gov.lk/media/DPA_Circular_012026_E.pdf); [Media.gov.lk](https://www.media.gov.lk/media-gallery/latest-news/3491-speaker-endorses-the-certificate-on-personal-data-protection-amendment-bill); [babl.ai](https://babl.ai/sri-lanka-delays-enforcement-of-data-protection-act-to-strengthen-regulatory-readiness/)

### Inferences
- In practice, OSA obligations on Trimegos are reactive: respond to a Commission notice or court order. With the Act in limbo, the safest path is a documented takedown/notice process with a named contact (trimegos@gmail.com), plus logging. That process works whatever replaces the OSA.
- PDPA core duties likely begin 1 Jan 2027, roughly two months after this research date. Verification video, face photos, DOB and gender are personal data, and some are arguably sensitive. Trimegos needs a PDPA compliance pass (lawful basis, DPO, retention, which already has the 7-day media purge, and breach handling) before or soon after launch. [LAWYER]

### Gaps
- OSA takedown timelines (e.g. 24-hour notices) and the exact definition of "internet intermediary": not verified from the Act text in this research. [LAWYER]
- Computer Crimes Act No. 24 of 2007 (offences, preservation orders), Telecommunications Act, Code of Criminal Procedure (production orders for user data), and the PDPA's disclosure exception for law enforcement: none retrieved. These are needed for a law-enforcement-requests policy. [LAWYER]
- Whether the 2024 OSA amendment was certified as an Act (and its number) is unresolved due to the source conflict above.

## 7. Emergency and incident-response resources

Confirmed numbers: Police emergency 119 (118 also listed), Police Bureau for women and children 109 (24h), Women's Helpline 1938 (Ministry of Women and Child Affairs, 24/7, Sinhala, Tamil and English), Child Helpline 1929, Women In Need (011) 471 8585, and National Mental Health Helpline 1926 (phone and text). Sumithrayo numbers conflict between directories. Not verified here: 1990 Suwa Seriya (widely known, but not re-checked in this pass), CERT "101", tourist police, and whether 119 accepts SMS.

### Cited Findings
- 1938: Women's Helpline run by the Ministry of Women and Child Affairs. 24/7, all three languages. — [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/115341/two-toll-free-hotlines-to-report-about-women-and-children-in-distress); [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/organizations/ministry-of-women-and-child-affairs-women-s-helpline)

## 8. Age and minors

### Takeaway
Minors are a real risk. The 2017 UNFPA harassment sample started at age 15, the Grindr extortion suspects were 17–20, and child cyber crime is rising. No Sri Lankan legal standard for online age verification was found. Bumble's BFF mode requires users to be 18+.

### Cited Findings
- Suspects in the Grindr extortion case were aged 17–20. — [Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/rznm)
- Cyber crimes targeting children are rising, and authorities are seeking awareness (Sept 2024). — [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/240901/news/as-cyber-crimes-targeting-children-rise-authorities-seek-to-raise-awareness-569961.html)
- NCPA Child Helpline 1929. — [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/115341/two-toll-free-hotlines-to-report-about-women-and-children-in-distress)
- Bumble BFF Community Guidelines require members to be 18+ with authentic, clear-face profiles. — [Bumble BFF safety page](https://bumble.com/bff/friendship/safe-online-friends); [Bumble safety](https://bumble.com/safety)

### Inferences
- Self-declared DOB (write-once) plus admin-reviewed live video is stronger than most peer apps. The video review should explicitly include an "appears under 18" rejection reason, with an escalation path to 1929 where there are signs of exploitation.

### Gaps
- Age of majority (18) and its statutory basis (Age of Majority Ordinance, as amended) were **not verified** in this research. [LAWYER]
- No Sri Lankan law or regulator guidance mandating age verification for apps was found. The OSA has child-related offences, but they were not reviewed.

## 9. Alcohol and injury

### Takeaway
Liquor sales are banned on every Poya (full moon) day and on certain other national days. Star-class hotels serving resident tourists are sometimes exempt. Enforcement is real (shops sealed). No source was found on a general public-drinking offence.

### Cited Findings
- The 2006 National Authority on Tobacco and Alcohol regime, as revised, bans liquor sales on Poya days. Penalties reported in 2009 included licence loss or fines of Rs 500,000. — [Sunday Times, 27 Dec 2009](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/091227/News/nws_05.html)
- The Excise Department lists all Poya days plus other national days as closure days each year. For 12–13 April 2025, the closure did not apply to resident tourists in hotels above three-star class. — [Daily Mirror](https://dailymirror.lk/front-page/April-12-13-dry-days-except-for-top-star-hotels/238-280363); [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news.php?nid=104979)
- A Ja-Ela liquor store was sealed for selling alcohol on Poya Day (2025). — [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news.php?nid=114396)
- Critics argue Poya bans create a black market. — [Daily FT editorial](https://www.ft.lk/FT-View-Editorial/Why-are-liquor-stores-closed-tomorrow/58-758092)

### Inferences
- Gig creation could warn when a "drinks" or "pub" gig falls on a Poya date (venues closed, a legal risk for hosts who bring alcohol to sell). A Poya calendar check in `Asia/Colombo` is cheap to add.
- Physical activities (futsal, hikes) carry injury risk. Terms should include assumption of risk. [LAWYER]

### Gaps
- Public-drinking law (drinking in public places, beaches, parks) and the legal drinking age (commonly cited as 21 under the NATA Act) were **not verified**.
- No data was found on activity injuries or hiking incidents (e.g. Knuckles, Adam's Peak rescues).

## 10. How comparable apps handle safety

### Takeaway
Only Bumble's materials were retrieved. They show photo verification, optional ID verification, 18+ for BFF, 24/7 moderation, reporting and bans, and pre-meet advice (video chat first, tell someone, call police). Timeleft and Meetup safety and law-enforcement pages were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Bumble offers photo verification (selfie matched to profile photos) and optional ID verification, and encourages verifying before meeting. — [Bumble support: Our safety features](https://support.bumble.com/hc/articles/28537051467293-Our-safety-features); [Bumble safety](https://bumble.com/safety)
- Bumble BFF uses a two-step check that people match their profile photos, follows the same moderation standards as the main app, and requires 18+ with a clear face photo. — [Bumble BFF](https://bumble.com/bff/friendship/safe-online-friends)
- Bumble has teams monitoring around the clock for fake profiles and guideline violations, and violations can lead to permanent bans. Its guidelines also cover no-shows at arranged meetups. — [Bumble: what gets you kicked off](https://bumble.com/the-buzz/what-will-get-you-kicked-off-bumble)
- Bumble launched a standalone BFF app in July 2023. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/26/bumble-is-a-launching-a-separate-bff-app-for-friend-friending)
- Australian eSafety maintains a Bumble safety guide (a regulator-facing example of a safety-centre page). — [eSafety](https://esafety.gov.au/key-issues/esafety-guide/bumble)

### Inferences
- Trimegos already matches or exceeds Bumble BFF on verification (live video with admin review, versus selfie match). Its gaps against peers are probably a public Safety Centre, a law-enforcement guidelines page, and pre-meet tips ("tell someone your plans").

### Gaps
- Timeleft (verification, venue model, safety), Meetup (Trust and Safety, law-enforcement guidelines), and Bumble's law-enforcement or transparency pages were not retrieved because of tool-call limits. Recommended follow-up: meetup.com/safety, help.meetup.com law enforcement guidelines, timeleft.com/faq or safety, bumble.com/en/law-enforcement.
