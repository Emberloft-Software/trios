# Sri Lankan local competitors and indirect alternatives (Colombo): activity partners and venue booking

All sources accessed 2026-10-31 unless noted. Play Store figures were read directly from the Google Play listing HTML (gl=LK) on 2026-10-31. "Not shown" means the listing showed no star rating, which Google hides until an app has enough ratings. Many local booking sites are JavaScript apps that show only a title to scrapers, so their own claims come from their meta descriptions or visible landing-page text. Treat all marketing claims as self-reported.

## Q1. Which local sports court/venue booking apps exist, and what do they offer (features, fees, Play Store rating/installs, last update, still active)?

### Takeaway
Colombo has a crowded, very young, fragmented field of court-booking products: at least 12 sites or apps, almost all launched 2024 to 2026 and almost all with tiny traction. The only local one with measurable traction is Play.Book (5K+ installs, 3.4 stars from 21 ratings). Most are venue-booking utilities. Only two (Volt, Play.Book) say they have social or "find players" features, and Volt's "post a game, fill the last slots" is the closest local overlap with Trimegos's futsal use case. No local player has scale. Several names from the brief (Sportify LK, Playmate, Kickoff, Courts.lk) could not be verified as Sri Lankan products. A "BookMyCourt" does exist, but it is a small 2026 app.

A separate venue app, "Play.Book Provider", has 100+ downloads and was updated Aug 13, 2026. Sources: [Play.Book on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lkdev.playbook.prod&gl=LK&hl=en); [Play.Book Provider](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lkdev.playbook.provider.prod&gl=LK&hl=en)

### Inferences
- The market is pre-consolidation: about 12 competing booking products, most web-only, and none with more than 5K installs. Venue booking is a commodity no one has won, and the real pain these products describe ("calling three venues", "the WhatsApp scrum") is coordination, which is Trimegos's space.
- Volt is the most direct local overlap: open games plus "fill the last slots from players nearby". It is sport-only and venue-led, though, with no social or non-sport activities, no minimum group size or safety framing, and no evidence yet that it has launched on an app store.
- Play.Book's 3.5% fee complaints and cash-at-venue promises (CricBook, BookMyCourt, Volt) suggest Sri Lankan players resist paying card or gateway fees for court booking. That matters if Trimegos ever handles payments.
- These could become partners rather than rivals: a futsal gig on Trimegos could link to a court booking on Volt, CricBook or Play.Book.

### Gaps
- No Play Store data for Volt, Sportizza, CricBook, MySport, BookMyPlay, GearUP or The Arena: no Android listings were found under those names. They may be web-only or listed under other names.
- No founding dates, funding or user numbers for any of them. None were covered in ReadMe.lk, Daily FT or EconomyNext results.
- No swimming-pool booking app found. Pools appear to be booked directly with hotels and clubs (not verified).
- iOS App Store listings were not checked.

## Q2. Local events/ticketing and social-discovery apps; Sri Lankan friend-finding or "meet new people" startups (past or present)

### Takeaway
Ticketing is mature and transactional (MyTickets.lk since 2014, BookMyShow LK with 50K+ installs, TicketGo), and none of it helps strangers form groups. In friend-finding, the most relevant local product is **mithuru** (mithuru.lk), a pre-launch Sri Lankan app announcing nearly the same "hangouts" idea plus campus circles and "Sports Connect". It is the closest direct local competitor and should be watched. Everything else is dating, chat-room or expat-matching sites. I found no documented Sri Lankan friend-finding startup that launched and then failed.

### Cited Findings
**Ticketing and event listings**
- **MyTickets.lk**: founded 2014, based in Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, a marketplace for movies, concerts, sports, seminars, exhibitions and festivals (per Tracxn). ZoomInfo estimates 11-50 staff and under $5M revenue (third-party estimate). — [Tracxn list](https://tracxn.com/d/explore/online-event-ticketing-startups-in-sri-lanka/__7Bhx4Kc7OLRj6URCKNh_KkDW5LXhEUltAtj0-bXw1J8/companies); [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/my-ticketslk/467193017); [mytickets.lk](https://mytickets.lk/)
- Tracxn counts **11 online event-ticketing startups in Sri Lanka**, including Wow.lk, Mytickets.lk and TicketsMinistry (Jan 2026). — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/explore/online-event-ticketing-startups-in-sri-lanka/__7Bhx4Kc7OLRj6URCKNh_KkDW5LXhEUltAtj0-bXw1J8/companies)
- **BookMyShow LK** (Bigtree Entertainment): Play Store **50K+ downloads, 3.95 stars from 479 ratings, updated Sep 4, 2026.** — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bt.bms.lk&gl=LK&hl=en)
- **TicketGo** calls itself "Sri Lanka's first entertainment-dedicated ticketing platform" (concerts, festivals, theatre, sports). — [ticketgo.lk](https://ticketgo.lk/)

Its 26 Oct to 28 Nov 2026 listings include social formats: "Board Game Night at Cafe Meeples, from 5.30pm to 11.00pm" (Fri 30 Oct), a quiz night at Cafe Meeples, "Midweek Boost" with board games, kottu and music at Third Place Hub, a Non Fiction Book Club and a Comic Arts Club at Lakmahal Library, a Death Cafe at Commons Coffeehouse, an Uno Brunch at Belle Colombo, and a murder mystery at Pixels and Pints (limit 20). **Friend-finding and social discovery**
- **mithuru (mithuru.lk)**: "find your people in sri lanka", "coming soon to the App Store", free with "no subscriptions, ads, or tracking", 18+, in English, Sinhala and Tamil. Announced features: scrapbook profiles; mutual "say hi" before chat opens; "Sidequests" (activity ideas such as kottu runs and sunset walks); **Hangouts** (example: sunset run at Port City Colombo, 5:30 pm, "6 people going"); **Campus circles** verified by campus email ("83 campuses and 38 work circles, and counting"); **Sports Connect** ("find sports partners and book courts"); **Apartment Connect** (utility-bill verification); and reports reviewed within 24h. It also explicitly includes "dates". No company or founders named. — [mithuru.lk](https://mithuru.lk/)
- **CeyLove**: "Sri Lankan Dating Site & Friends App", says it covers all 25 districts. — [ceylove.com](https://ceylove.com/)
- **Wooh**: a friend-matching app ("Once per week get only 1 new friend to meet", "No Swipes Only IRL Meetings", photo shown only after mutual acceptance). It has a Colombo expat landing page, but the page reads as programmatic SEO and gives no evidence of actual Colombo users. — [wooh.app/sri-lanka/colombo](https://wooh.app/sri-lanka/colombo)
- **Random Chat Sri Lanka** (CodeScale LK, Android) and chat-room sites such as srilankanchat.com and tochato.com offer anonymous chat, not offline meetups. — [Softonic listing](https://random-chat-sri-lanka.en.softonic.com/android); [srilankanchat.com](https://srilankanchat.com/)
- **Lanka Link** (Blitzify Labs) targets Sri Lankans in the UK (100+ downloads, updated May 12, 2026), so it is not a Colombo competitor. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lankalink.app&gl=LK&hl=en)
- A boo.world article (written by a competing app, so biased) says that in Sri Lanka "dating apps are popular", but there are very few options for people looking to make friends. — [boo.world](https://boo.world/resources/how-to-make-friends-in-sri-lanka)

### Inferences
- mithuru is the biggest local direct-competition risk: it targets the same "meet in person around plans" idea with Sri Lankan cultural framing (poya days, kottu runs, trilingual) and stronger community hooks (campus and apartment verification). Unlike Trimegos, it bundles "dates", so Trimegos's "not dating, min 3, public places" position is a clear differentiator against it.
- Event-listing sources (Happening Colombo, Instagram) show demand for social formats such as board games, quiz, book clubs and run clubs. They are organiser-led, though: a solo person still has to go alone, which is the gap Trimegos's crew model fills.

### Gaps
- mithuru's team, launch date and funding are unknown; it is not yet on Google Play (a Play search for "mithuru" found only an unrelated "Govimithuru").
- No documented failed Sri Lankan friend-finding startups were found. A ReadMe.lk-targeted search returned nothing relevant.
- No traffic or user numbers for MyTickets, TicketGo or Happening Colombo.

## Q3. Are Meetup, Timeleft, Bumble (BFF), Playo, Hudle actually used in Colombo?

### Takeaway
Mostly no. Meetup in Colombo is almost entirely tech user groups, and its only general outdoor/social group has 378 members and no upcoming events. Timeleft does not operate in Colombo. Playo and Hudle show no Sri Lankan presence. Bumble is available in Sri Lanka but there is no public evidence of its local scale, and the standalone BFF app had not launched in Sri Lanka.

### Cited Findings
- **Meetup, Colombo groups list (15 groups shown, 2026-10-31):** AWS User Group Colombo **4,839 members**; Business Networking for you 2,438; KSUG.AI Sri Lanka 1,746; LFDT Sri Lanka 1,381; Flutter Colombo 1,252; Sri Lankan Microsoft BizApps User Group 1,199; Platform Engineers Colombo 1,172; Sahaja Yoga Meditation Colombo 871; The AI Meetup Sri Lanka 446. The rest are AWS student builder groups (about 300 each), OpenSearch (134) and Grafana & Friends (125). — [Meetup find: Colombo groups](https://www.meetup.com/find/?location=lk--Colombo&source=GROUPS)
- Keyword searches for "make friends", "hiking", "board games", "expats", "running" and "football" in Colombo all returned the same tech groups plus one social group: **Colombo outdoor adventures Meetup Group, 378 members, founded 2025-01-01, 0 upcoming events listed** (no past events were returned either). — [Meetup search: hiking, Colombo](https://www.meetup.com/find/?location=lk--Colombo&source=GROUPS&keywords=hiking&distance=tenMiles); [group page](https://www.meetup.com/colombo-outdoor-adventures-meetup-group/)

- **Hudle**: hudle.in showed no mention of Colombo or Sri Lanka (2026-10-31). — [hudle.in](https://www.hudle.in/)

### Inferences
- The "global format, absent locally" gap is real: Timeleft (dinners with strangers) has reached Mumbai, Bangalore and Dubai but not Colombo, and Meetup has no social or activity community there. Colombo has no incumbent "join strangers for an activity" platform.
- Meetup's Colombo pattern (tech and professional groups in the thousands, social groups near zero) suggests Colombo residents use platforms for professional networking, while social plans run through WhatsApp and Instagram.

### Gaps
- No Sri Lanka-specific usage data (MAU, download rank) for Bumble, Tinder or Bumble BFF mode; app-intelligence data (Sensor Tower, data.ai) was not accessible.
- Could not confirm whether the Meetup outdoor group ever held an event.

## Q4. Indirect alternatives: Facebook groups, WhatsApp Channels, Instagram communities, clubs/leagues, corporate sports, university societies; "doing nothing"

These work well for people already inside a circle and poorly for newcomers, people whose friends have scattered, or anyone wanting a one-off small-group plan. Member counts for Facebook groups and WhatsApp Channels could not be retrieved; Facebook blocks scraping and search engines did not index the counts.

### Strengths and weaknesses as ways to find people (Inferences)
| Channel | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Personal WhatsApp groups | Free, universal, instant, trusted | Closed to outsiders; "scrum" to fill last slots; no discovery, no safety layer, chats go dead |
| WhatsApp Channels | Broadcast reach | One-way; can't form a group of 3-6 strangers |
| Facebook groups / venue pages | Large reach, public, searchable | Noisy; older-skewing; no headcount or confirmation; scams and spam; no verification |
| Instagram communities (run clubs, café nights) | Trendy, recurring, free, low commitment | Big-crowd format (you still arrive alone); organiser-dependent; discovery through follows only |
| Event listings / ticketing | Curated, timely | Transactional; doesn't match you with people |
| Mercantile and corporate leagues | Structured, regular, strong social glue | Only for employees of member firms; team-level, not individual; competitive |
| University societies / teams | Easy entry for students | Ends at graduation; campus-bounded |
| Meetup | Event mechanics built in | Dead for social use in Colombo (tech only) |
| Booking apps (Volt, Play.Book) | Solve the court; some "fill slots" features | Sport-only; tiny user bases; fee friction |
| "Doing nothing" | Zero effort, zero risk | Loneliness; the default for newcomers and people whose friends have moved abroad |

### Gaps

- No Instagram follower counts beyond Colombo Night Run (and that one came from a search snippet).
- No data quantifying "doing nothing" (e.g. Sri Lankan loneliness or social-isolation surveys); this needs a separate search.

## Q5. Local review themes: Play Store 1-3 star reviews for local apps

### Takeaway
Few local sports apps have enough reviews to analyse; only Play.Book has visible reviews. The recurring complaints are **fees (3.5% gateway charge), no cash option, and slow payouts**, and for BookMyShow LK, **payment bugs, sign-in failures and forced location selection**. Sri Lankan users react badly to card-only payments and surcharges.

### Cited Findings
- **Play.Book, 2 stars:** the reviewer complains that payment gateways add 3.5% and bank deposits take 24 hours, and calls the process "old school". — [Play.Book reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lkdev.playbook.prod&gl=LK&hl=en)
- **Play.Book, 1 star:** fine as a booking app, but no cash and a 3.5% booking fee, which the reviewer calls "extortion". — [same](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lkdev.playbook.prod&gl=LK&hl=en)
- **Play.Book, 5 stars:** convenient booking at your own time. — [same](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lkdev.playbook.prod&gl=LK&hl=en)
- **BookMyShow LK (featured reviews):** card CVV keyboard fails on QuickPay; the FriMi payment "OK" button stays disabled; choosing a location is a nuisance in a small country; booking confirmation is slow and in-app sign-in shows a blank "getting started" screen; theatre-only discounts (senior citizens) are missing in the app. — [BookMyShow LK on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bt.bms.lk&gl=LK&hl=en)
- BookMyCourt, Champ Turf and Play.Book Provider showed no reviews or ratings on their listings (too few). — [BookMyCourt](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bookmycourt.app&gl=LK&hl=en); [Champ Turf](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.treinetic.championsturf.app&gl=LK&hl=en)

### Inferences
- Offer cash-at-venue or split-in-person options and avoid surcharges. Trimegos not handling money at all is an advantage worth stating.
- Local payment rails (FriMi, eZ Cash, PayHere, QuickPay) are fragile in apps, and users notice. Keep Trimegos's core flow free of payments.
- Little review data means local competitors have very few active users. That is weak evidence, but consistent with the install bands (100+ to 5K+).

### Gaps
- Only the Play Store's default "featured" reviews were visible (about 3 per app); the full review sets were not accessible without the Play API.
- No App Store (iOS) reviews checked.
