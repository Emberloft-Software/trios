# Problem and demand: group-activity meetups for 18 to 35 year-olds in Colombo

All URLs accessed 2026-10-31. Items marked "(snippet)" come from a search-result summary where the page itself could not be opened; verify them before quoting in the final report. Reddit (reddit.com) is blocked to both the search and fetch tools used here, so no Reddit threads could be captured (see Gaps under Q1).

## Q1. How often do young people in Colombo want company for activities, and how severe is the gap?

### Takeaway
No Sri Lankan dataset measures how often 18 to 35 year-olds want, or fail to find, company for activities. The nearest proxies are adolescent loneliness figures (about 1 in 5 school students lonely in 2024) and the visible existence of many volunteer-run activity communities in Colombo. Frequency and severity have to come from Trimegos's own primary research.

### Cited Findings
- No national youth survey, DCS publication, UNFPA, LIRNEasia or CPA output turned up a question on "wanting company for an activity" or "lacking people to do things with" for Sri Lankan adults. Searches for a national youth loneliness survey returned only school-age GSHS analyses and a scale-validation study ([search: ResearchGate DJGLS-11 validation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405100917_VALIDATION_OF_A_SCALE_DJGLS-11_FOR_ASSESSING_LONELINESS_AMONG_SCHOOL-GOING_ADOLESCENTS_IN_SRI_LANKA)). The validation paper's authors themselves note a lack of validated, culturally appropriate loneliness instruments for Sri Lankan adolescents (snippet).
- Proxy, adolescents 13 to 17 (GSHS 2024, via WHO Sri Lanka, 10 Sep 2025): "more than one in five" reported loneliness; almost 18% persistent depression; 15% seriously considered suicide in the past year; "nearly one in ten" attempted, higher among girls — [WHO Sri Lanka, World Suicide Prevention Day 2025](https://www.who.int/srilanka/news/detail/10-09-2025-world-suicide-prevention-day-2025--changing-the-narrative-on-suicide-in-sri-lanka)
- Proxy, global 13 to 29 year-olds: 17 to 21% report feeling lonely (WHO Commission on Social Connection, 30 Jun 2025) — [WHO news release](https://www.who.int/news/item/30-06-2025-social-connection-linked-to-improved-heath-and-reduced-risk-of-early-death)

### Inferences
- If the 2024 GSHS cohort (13 to 17) carries its loneliness into early adulthood, a meaningful share of Trimegos's 18 to 24 segment will already have reported loneliness as teenagers. This is an inference only; no longitudinal SL data was found.

### Gaps
- Frequency of "wanted to do X but had nobody to go with" in the last month, by age, gender, district and migrant status. Primary research needed: a short survey (n of about 300+, Colombo, 18 to 35) asking (a) how many times in the last 30 days they skipped or postponed an activity for lack of company, (b) which activities, (c) where they currently look for people, (d) safety concerns about meeting strangers, by gender.
- No SL data on adult (18+) loneliness prevalence at all. Gallup/Meta's 142-country 2023 study may contain a Sri Lanka row on its interactive map; not checked (see Q6).

## Q2. Which online channels (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, Meetup) do people in Colombo use to find activity partners or friends?

### Takeaway
Discovery is fragmented across Instagram accounts, Facebook pages, Meetup.com groups and WhatsApp groups run by each community. Meetup.com in Colombo is dominated by tech groups; the social and hobby groups found are small (hundreds to about 2,000 members). Generic "make friends" WhatsApp link directories exist but are spam-heavy and often adult-themed, which is itself a trust and safety signal.

### Cited Findings
**Meetup.com (Colombo)**
- Of 14 groups on Meetup's Colombo groups listing, 12 are tech or professional (e.g. AWS User Group Colombo 4,839 members; Business Networking for you 2,438; LFDT Sri Lanka 1,381; Flutter Colombo 1,252); the only non-tech ones were Sahaja Yoga Meditation Colombo (871) — [Meetup Colombo groups listing](https://www.meetup.com/find/?location=lk--Colombo&source=GROUPS)
- Meetup's Colombo "outdoor activities" page listed mostly tech events mis-tagged as hiking/running, one touring event ("Sri Lanka - Temples, Tea, & Timeless Beauty", Adventures and Events, Sat Dec 5), and "suggested groups" from Hong Kong, Hyderabad, Bangkok and Nagpur, i.e. no active Colombo outdoor-social Meetup group surfaced — [Meetup Colombo outdoor activities](https://www.meetup.com/find/lk--colombo/outdoor-activities/)
- "Colombo Social": 690 members, describes itself as a community for young Sri Lankans to socialise and support personal growth (also mental health awareness); only 3 past events, the last being "Rawula - මචංhood Colombo Catchup" on Fri 24 May 2024 at Hangla, One Galle Face Mall; no upcoming events — [Meetup: Colombo Social](https://www.meetup.com/colombo-social/)
- "The Fun Boardgames" (Colombo) asks people to follow Instagram @thefunboardgames and join a WhatsApp group; gamemasters teach newcomers (snippet) — [Meetup: The Fun Boardgames](https://www.meetup.com/the-fun-boardgames/)

**Instagram**
- Tabletop SL (@tabletop_sl), Colombo board-gaming community: about 2,441 followers (snippet) — [Instagram @tabletop_sl](https://www.instagram.com/tabletop_sl/). A search snippet also showed its Meetup group with about 2,078 members, but both Meetup URLs found (`/tabletop-ceylon/`, `/tabletop-sl/`) now return "group not found" — [Meetup tabletop-ceylon](https://www.meetup.com/tabletop-ceylon/); [Meetup tabletop-sl](https://www.meetup.com/tabletop-sl/). A 2023 event page existed: "Boardgaming Meetup #33", Sun 8 Oct 2023 (snippet) — [Meetup event](https://www.meetup.com/tabletop-sl/events/296466123/). **Facebook pages (likes, not group membership; values from search snippets)**

**WhatsApp**
- Web directories of Sri Lankan "make friends" WhatsApp group links exist, but results were unvetted and mixed with adult/spam content (e.g. [dpg-shop.schreiner-group.com listing](https://dpg-shop.schreiner-group.com/by6bl/whatsapp-group-links-18-sri-lanka-2018.html)). Not citable as a demand measure; useful only as evidence that open WhatsApp groups are an unsafe channel.
- Community-run WhatsApp groups are the standard follow-up channel for hobby groups (e.g. The Fun Boardgames, above). Volt, a Sri Lankan sports-booking startup, markets itself as "End the WhatsApp scrum" and depicts a venue owner juggling "3 WhatsApp groups, 1 notebook" — [Volt](https://www.volt.lk/)

- Luma is also used for professional meetups in Sri Lanka (e.g. "WorkInSriLanka May Meet-up") — [Luma](https://luma.com/o6tywi5j)

### Inferences
- The absence of any active general "do activities with new people" Meetup or Facebook community in Colombo (as far as indexable sources show), alongside many single-activity communities, suggests the cross-activity, small-group format Trimegos offers is unserved rather than proven to be in demand.
- Colombo Social (690 members, 3 events, dormant since May 2024) and the Tabletop SL Meetup group disappearing suggest volunteer-run social groups struggle to sustain organiser effort, which supports a "any member can host" model.
- Spam and adult content in open WhatsApp link directories supports Trimegos's positioning on verification and public places.

### Gaps
- **Reddit:** reddit.com is not accessible to the research tools (search returned an explicit block). No r/srilanka or r/colombo thread titles, dates or URLs could be captured. Action: a team member should manually search r/srilanka and r/colombo for "friends", "lonely", "new to Colombo", "futsal players", "hiking buddies", "board games" and log title, date, URL and comment count.
- **Facebook groups** (as opposed to pages): member counts for groups such as Colombo expat groups, futsal/badminton player groups or hiking groups are not indexed. Manual check needed while logged in.
- InterNations Colombo: nothing indexable found; size unknown.
- Instagram follower counts beyond @tabletop_sl were not captured.

## Q3. Which clubs and organised communities exist in Colombo, and how big and active are they?

### Takeaway
Colombo has a healthy layer of free, recurring, volunteer-run activity communities (running, hashing, cycling, board games) that meet weekly or fortnightly. Public size data is thin; the best figures are Tabletop SL's growth from 10 people (2019) to 400+ members (2023) and Colombo Night Run drawing members from 43 countries.

### Cited Findings
**Running**
- Colombo Night Run (CNR): first run 9 June 2016; weekly Thursday 7:15 pm at the D.S. Senanayake statue, Independence Square, run starts 7:30 pm; members from 43 countries on 6 continents; no headcount published — [CNR About](https://www.colombonightrun.com/about). Described as non-profit and for all levels; free, untimed 5K, not a race (listing dated 30 Jan 2025) — [ahotu event listing](https://www.ahotu.com/event/weekly-night-5k-by-colombo-night-run-club); [Timeoutdoors](https://www.timeoutdoors.com/clubs/colombo-night-run)
- Lightning ("Lightning by Neesh"): weekly Sunday 7 am run from Peppermint Café, pacer-led, café social afterwards; founder moved back from the UK. Feature dated 7 May 2026 describes the main barrier as feeling one does not "belong" among athletes, which the group dissolved quickly; no attendance figures — [The Sun.lk / Solar HQ, 7 May 2026](https://www.thesun.lk/solar_hq/Why-is-everyone-Running-Now-Inside-Colombo-s-Super-Fun-Run-Club/557-305220)

**Hash House Harriers**
- Colombo Hash House Harriers (men only) and Colombo Hash House Harriettes (women; tradition dating to 1984); weekly Saturday walk/run from 5 pm at varying Colombo/suburb locations followed by drinks — [Colombo Hash Club](https://www.colombohash.org/); [Colombo Hash House Harriettes](https://www.hashcolombo.com/); [Happening Colombo](https://www.happeningcolombo.com/offer-by-type/other-events); [Hash magazine listing](http://www.colombohash.com/aggregator/sources/4?page=1)

**Board games**
- Tabletop Sri Lanka: founded by Milinda Dias; first public meetup December 2019 with 10 people; paused about two years during the pandemic, resumed late 2021; 400+ members by Sept 2023; run by a team of five; every two weeks on Sundays 1:30 to 7:30 pm at Brew 1867 by Dilmah (capacity about 75, tables of 10 to 15); free; 18+; first visit walk-in, then RSVP via Meetup; the article frames it as a way to make new friends and get past discomfort talking to strangers — [Sunday Times, 10 Sep 2023](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/230910/magazine/board-games-going-back-in-time-but-with-a-twist-531640.html)
- The Fun Boardgames (Meetup, Instagram, WhatsApp) — see Q2.

**Cycling** (data mostly 2012 to 2016)
- Pedal Pushers (formed 2010; weeknight ride from Independence Square to Fort; weekend off-road rides 6:30 am); organised the Colombo Cycle Fun Ride, 300+ participants in 2016 (5th edition). Other groups named: Free Wheelers, Wroom, BoraluBoyz, Action Lanka, Colombo Mountain Bikers (snippet) — [Echelon: Biking buddies](https://echelon.lk/biking-buddies); [Daily FT, 2016](https://www.ft.lk/sports/colombo-cycling-fun-ride-2016-an-overwhelming-success/23-532553)

**Futsal and court sports (venue and booking layer)**
- Colombo Futsal Club: described in 2014 as a JATCO (Dubai) and local partnership; claims to be the only international-standard indoor soccer facility in Sri Lanka — [Roar Media archive, 2014](https://archive.roar.media/english/life/startups/keep-calm-play-futsalat-colombo-futsal-club); [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/colombofutsalclub/)
- Booking/directory apps: Turfify lists 9 futsal venues in Colombo — [Turfify](https://turfify.theatom.lk/sports/futsal/colombo); Bookin360 lists 19 futsal listings — [Bookin360](https://bookin360.com/); also Turfy ([bookturfy.com](https://bookturfy.com/)), Sportizza ([sportizza.com](https://sportizza.com/)), Champ Turf ([champturf.com](https://champturf.com/)). These book courts; none was confirmed to match individual players.
- Volt (volt.lk): presents itself as launching in 2026; venue-management plus player app covering cricket, tennis, badminton, futsal, basketball, padel, squash, swimming (and pickleball); player app lets you "post a game, see who's in, and fill the last slots" from nearby players; "Founding 50" venue offer before 31 July 2026; no user numbers published (dashboard figures are mock-ups) — [Volt](https://www.volt.lk/). This is the closest direct overlap with Trimegos for sports gigs.

**Other**
- Global Shapers Colombo Hub: 25 members (a membership hub, not open social) — [Global Shapers](https://www.globalshapers.org:443/hubs/colombo-hub)

### Inferences
- Existing communities are activity-specific and schedule-fixed (Thursday 7:15 pm, every other Sunday). Someone who wants a hike this Saturday, or a 4th for badminton tonight, has no general channel. That ad-hoc gap is where Trimegos fits.
- Several communities already practise Trimegos-like norms (free, newcomer-friendly, hosts/pacers assigned to welcome strangers, 18+), which suggests these norms are culturally accepted in Colombo.
- Volt is a direct competitor for "fill the last slots" in sports and should go into the competitor research.

### Gaps
- Current headcounts and weekly attendance for CNR, Lightning, the Hash groups and Tabletop SL. Primary research: attend one session of each and count, or ask organisers.
- Colombo Toastmasters clubs, InterNations Colombo, badminton/pickleball social groups and futsal leagues: no indexable data found.
- Age profile of members in all of the above.

## Q4. What Sri Lankan data exists on loneliness, social isolation and youth mental health, including post-2022 crisis effects?

### Takeaway
Hard SL data covers adolescents (GSHS) only: loneliness among school students ranges from about 10% to 31% depending on survey and definition, with GSHS 2024 at "more than one in five". For 18 to 35 year-olds there is no loneliness prevalence data; crisis-era evidence is polls and commentary, not prevalence studies.

### Cited Findings
- GSHS 2024 (ages 13 to 17): >1 in 5 lonely; ~18% persistent depression; 15% seriously considered suicide; ~1 in 10 attempted — [WHO Sri Lanka, 10 Sep 2025](https://www.who.int/srilanka/news/detail/10-09-2025-world-suicide-prevention-day-2025--changing-the-narrative-on-suicide-in-sri-lanka)
- Sri Lanka suicide rate 15 per 100,000 (2022 police data); men far more affected — same WHO page.
- GSHS 2016 secondary analysis (3,262 students, grades 8 to 13): 30.8% (95% CI 29.3 to 32.5) perceived loneliness; associated with reduced parental support, bullying, truancy, fights (snippet) — [Rasalingam et al., Health Science Reports, 2022](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/hsr2.886). Other summaries of the same survey cite about 10% (snippet), so figures depend on the loneliness threshold used.
- DJGLS-11 Sinhala loneliness scale validation: 39.2% of school-going adolescents in the pilot reported moderate to high loneliness (snippet) — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405100917_VALIDATION_OF_A_SCALE_DJGLS-11_FOR_ASSESSING_LONELINESS_AMONG_SCHOOL-GOING_ADOLESCENTS_IN_SRI_LANKA)
- 2025 survey of grade 10 to 13 students in seven Gampaha District schools on common mental health problems, framed against conflict, the 2019 attacks and the economic crisis (snippet; figures not retrieved) — [PMC12509160](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12509160/)
- UNICEF U-Report poll (May to July 2022): 68% of young respondents said they were "very much" affected by the crisis; impact higher among 20 to 29 year-olds than teenagers; mental stress ranked third (10%) after money worries and studies (snippet) — [UNICEF Sri Lanka U-Report poll report](https://www.unicef.org/srilanka/media/3041/file/Youth%20experiences%20during%20crisis%20May%20July%202022.pdf)
- UNICEF 2023 situation report: anecdotal partner reports of rising mental health problems; 952,551 adolescents reached with school-based psychosocial support (snippet) — [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sri-lanka/unicef-sri-lanka-humanitarian-situation-report-no-2-economic-crisis-january-december-2023)
- A 2022/23 Lancet Regional Health SE Asia commentary calls for action on child, adolescent and youth mental health after violence, COVID-19 and the crisis; it is a call to action, not a prevalence study — [PMC10306051](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10306051/) (full text blocked by captcha; content from search summary)
- Sri Lanka Sumithrayo provides emotional support "to those who are lonely, depressed, despairing" (helpline 011 2696666); no 2024 to 2025 call-volume or youth-loneliness data found — [findahelpline Sri Lanka loneliness](https://findahelpline.com/countries/lk/topics/loneliness); [Sunday Times, 2016](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/160619/plus/sri-lanka-sumithrayo-42-years-of-reaching-out-to-those-in-need-197548.html)
- "Let's TALK & Walk 2025" (CCC Foundation and 1333 Crisis Support Line), a youth-focused mental health walk for World Mental Health Day, 10 Oct 2025 — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/news/Lets-TALK-Walk-2025-A-National-Call-to-Break-the-Silence-on-Mental-Health/239-321779)

### Inferences
- The consistent 20 to 30% adolescent loneliness range across GSHS rounds suggests loneliness is not a fringe issue for the cohort now entering Trimegos's age band. Do not present adolescent figures as adult figures.
- Crisis-era evidence shows young adults (20 to 29) felt the crisis more than teens, so they have less money for paid socialising. That supports free, cheap gigs (walks, runs, board games at a café).

### Gaps
- No national adult or youth (18 to 35) loneliness prevalence. No National Youth Survey round newer than about 2013 was found (not searched in depth). LIRNEasia, CPA and UNFPA Sri Lanka outputs on youth social isolation were not found in searches.
- Sumithrayo and 1333 call statistics by age.
- Effect of remote/hybrid work on socialising in Sri Lanka: no data found.

## Q5. Emigration and brain drain after the 2022 crisis

### Takeaway
Labour emigration is at record highs: about 311,000 departures in 2025 after a record of about 313,000 to 315,000 in 2024, with a 2026 target of 310,000 to 350,000. Survey data (2021) shows about half of 18 to 29 year-olds want to migrate. For Trimegos this means friend groups get hollowed out as peers leave.

### Cited Findings
- SLBFE departures: 310,915 in 2025; 314,672 in 2024 (record); 2026 target 350,000; remittances about US$7.8bn in 2025 (record) — [Daily Mirror, 6 Jan 2026](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Sri-Lanka-eyes-350-000-foreign-employment-departures-in-2026/108-329575)
- Conflicting 2024 figures: 312,836 per The Morning (Jan 2025) and 314,668 per RTI data; 2025 also reported as 311,190 or 311,207 (snippets) — [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/ux1e2iNAmWnmebhctASm); [Ada Derana](https://adaderana.lk/news/114084). SLBFE target cited elsewhere as 310,000 for 2026 — [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news.php?nid=117681)
- Interim 2025: 144,379 in H1; 212,302 in Jan to Aug (snippet) — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/news/Over-212-000-Sri-Lankans-depart-for-foreign-jobs-in-first-eight-months/239-318967)
- Census 2024: 672,249 people belong to households and are temporarily residing abroad; 61.3% male — [DCS Census 2024 press release (final report)](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/qlink/PressReleases/press_note_CPH_2024_En)
- Migration intention (IHP Sri Lanka Opinion Tracker Survey 2021): about 48% of 18 to 29 year-olds want to leave; about 53% of degree holders and 45% of A/L holders (snippet; pre-dates 2022 crisis peak) — [Daily Mirror: Increased migration of educated youth](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/business-news/Increased-migration-of-educated-youth/273-285284); [Asia Research News](https://www.asiaresearchnews.com/content/youth-migration-challenges-and-opportunities-sri-lanka-0)
- 2023 field survey of undergraduates and fresh graduates: 67% had strong to very strong intention to migrate; family ties the main deterrent (snippet) — [University of Moratuwa repository](https://dl.lib.uom.lk/items/88ebaae4-1637-445c-970f-fa1285e3f821)
- Counterpoint: one source notes departures among 15 to 29 year-olds relative to their population had not risen noticeably, i.e. intention does not equal departures (snippet) — [Asia Research News](https://www.asiaresearchnews.com/content/youth-migration-challenges-and-opportunities-sri-lanka-0)

### Inferences
- Large-scale outflow of young, educated people means those who stay lose friends. That creates a recurring need to rebuild social circles, which a low-commitment meetup format serves. Not directly measured; frame as a hypothesis.

### Gaps
- SLBFE age breakdown of departures (share aged 18 to 35) and professional/skilled share for 2025 to 2026 not retrieved; check SLBFE annual statistical report.
- Non-SLBFE emigration (students, PR migrants to Australia/Canada/UK) is not captured in SLBFE figures; no consolidated number found.
- Post-2022 national migration-intention figure (newer SLOTS rounds) not retrieved.

## Q6. Internal migration to Colombo: census, labour force, IT/BPM workforce, students

### Takeaway
Colombo and Gampaha districts are Sri Lanka's two big net magnets for internal migrants: about 0.5 million in-migrants each by previous residence, and net lifetime in-migration of +191,524 for Colombo and +357,528 for Gampaha (Census 2024). A large IT/BPM workforce (around 150,000 to 175,000, mostly Colombo-based) and university students add a pool of young people living away from home networks.

### Cited Findings
- Census 2024 (census moment 19 Dec 2024; final-report press note): population 21,781,800; Western Province 28.1% of total; Gampaha most populous district (2,436,142); median age 35; lifetime migrants 13% of population; net lifetime in-migration highest in Gampaha (+357,528) and Colombo (+191,524); by previous district of residence, Gampaha and Colombo each have over 0.5 million in-migrants, and Colombo also has high out-migration; Kandy, Matara, Nuwara Eliya and Galle have the highest net lifetime out-migration; unemployment 6% overall, highest among 20 to 24 year-olds — [DCS Census 2024 press release](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/qlink/PressReleases/press_note_CPH_2024_En)
- Colombo District population 2,374,461 with the highest density (3,549 per km²) per the preliminary report (snippet); citypopulation.de gives 2,375,415 — [DCS CPH 2024 preliminary report](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/Population/CPH_2024/CPH2024_Preliminary_Report.pdf); [citypopulation.de](https://www.citypopulation.de/en/srilanka/prov/admin/western/11__colombo/). Note: one search summary wrongly said Colombo had the largest net in-migration; the official note ranks Gampaha first.
- Unofficial comparative report: about 3.17 million migrants (14.5%); main reasons marriage, employment and family; flows towards Colombo, Gampaha and Kandy (not an official DCS publication; use with care) (snippet) — [eLanka comparative report, Nov 2025](https://www.elanka.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Comprehensive-Comparative-Report-on-the-2012-and-2024-Censuses-of-Population-and-Housing-in-Sri-Lanka_251102_132333.pdf)
- LFS 2025 annual: LFPR 49.4%, unemployment 3.9%, employed 8,079,040 — [DCS LFS Annual Bulletin 2025](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/LabourForce/Bulletins/LFS_Annual%20Bulletin_2025.pdf). Youth (15 to 24) unemployment 20.8% in Q2 2025 (men 17.5%, women 26.2%) — [Hiru News](https://hirunews.lk/english/business/425124/lankas-youth-unemployment-hits-20-8-in-q2-2025-highest-among-all-age-groups)
- IT/BPM workforce: about 175,000 professionals (Daily FT, July 2026) (snippet) — [Daily FT: SLASSCOM reflects on a year](https://www.ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/SLASSCOM-reflects-on-a-year-of-creating-opportunities-strengthening-Sri-Lanka-s-knowledge-and-innovation-industry/50-794672); about 150,000 direct and 250,000 indirect (Sunday Observer, March 2025) (snippet) — [Sunday Observer](https://www.sundayobserver.lk/2025/03/17/business/47627/slasscom-targets-us5b-revenue). Baseline: ICTA National IT-BPM Workforce Survey 2019 — [ICTA PDF](https://www.icta.lk/icta-assets/uploads/2023/02/NATIONAL-IT-BPM-WORKFORCE-SURVEY-2019-1-1.pdf). A 2014 report said most IT/BPM firms were Colombo-based, with plans for 50% to move outside Colombo — [Sunday Times, 2014](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/141102/business-times/50-of-sls-itbpm-companies-to-move-outside-colombo-125226.html)
- University students: UGC publishes "Undergraduate Admissions by District" tables yearly (e.g. 2020/21), and University of Colombo had 10,197 undergraduates in 2020 (snippet); no district-of-origin or boarding figures retrieved — [UGC Statistics 2024 Ch.1](https://www.ugc.ac.lk/downloads/statistics/stat_2024/Chapter%201.pdf); [UGC Statistics 2020 Ch.1](https://www.ugc.ac.lk/downloads/statistics/stat_2020/Chapter%201.pdf)

### Inferences
- Over 1 million recent in-migrants into Colombo plus Gampaha (the Colombo metro area) are the people most likely to lack local friends. Many will be young workers and students, but the census note gives no age breakdown for migrants.
- The IT/BPM workforce (young, urban, digitally fluent, often from outside Colombo) is a clear early-adopter segment to test.

### Gaps

## Q7. Global evidence on loneliness and demand for friend-making and activity apps (context only)

### Takeaway
WHO (2025) puts loneliness at 1 in 6 people worldwide and 17 to 21% of 13 to 29 year-olds, with loneliness in low-income countries double that in high-income ones. Friend-making products show real but fragile demand: Timeleft reports large scale but had to pull back from low-density cities; Bumble BFF has no disclosed traction or revenue.

### Cited Findings
- WHO Commission on Social Connection (30 Jun 2025): 1 in 6 people affected by loneliness; about 871,000 deaths a year (about 100 per hour); 17 to 21% of 13 to 29 year-olds lonely, highest among teens; about 24% lonely in low-income countries versus about 11% in high-income countries; social isolation affects up to 1 in 3 older adults and 1 in 4 adolescents; recommends joining local groups and volunteering — [WHO news release](https://www.who.int/news/item/30-06-2025-social-connection-linked-to-improved-heath-and-reduced-risk-of-early-death); [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2025/06/1165101)
- Gallup/Meta Global State of Social Connections (2023, 142 countries): nearly 1 in 4 people feel very or fairly lonely; 49% not lonely at all — [Gallup](https://news.gallup.com/opinion/gallup/512618/almost-quarter-world-feels-lonely.aspx); [report PDF](https://gallup.com/file/analytics/513347/Gallup-Meta-Global%20State%20of%20Social%20Connections%20Report-2023.pdf). Earlier 7-country study (2022, about 14,000 respondents): just under half reported some loneliness; India highest at nearly 60% (snippet) — [Gallup study page](https://www.gallup.com/analytics/402911/state-of-social-connections-study.aspx)
- Timeleft (dinners with strangers): founder interview reports €18M ARR and 150k monthly participants in August 2025 after scaling back to 200+ cities, having opened 325 cities in one year; many smaller cities fizzled for lack of participant density — [Tim Frin Substack](https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-timelefts-journey-to-connecting). Earlier: 11,500+ strangers every Wednesday (Feb 2025) — [Alta](https://www.altaonline.com/dispatches/a63924701/strangers-in-the-night/); about 27K participants a week (Apr 2025) — [Raleigh Magazine](https://raleighmag.com/2025/04/29/timeleft-raleigh-app/). Self-reported 3M+ users on Google Play — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.timeleft.app&hl=en). Alta reports the founder's first idea (matching strangers for shared activities like skydiving) did not take off, while low-stakes group meals did — [Alta](https://www.altaonline.com/dispatches/a63924701/strangers-in-the-night/)
- Bumble BFF relaunched Sept 2025 on the Geneva platform (acquired 2024), adding group discovery, communities and events; US first; no BFF user numbers disclosed; BFF/Geneva had generated no revenue as of Q3 2025 — [TechCrunch, 18 Sep 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building); [Sherwood News](https://www.sherwood.news/business/bumble-looks-to-friendship-to-reinvigorate-its-stock-price/)

### Inferences
- WHO's low-income versus high-income gap (24% vs 11%) places lower-middle-income Sri Lanka in the higher-loneliness band. This is context, not a Sri Lankan measurement.
- The Timeleft lessons (density kills or makes a city; low-stakes formats beat ambitious activities) argue for launching Trimegos in a few Colombo neighbourhoods with low-stakes default gigs (coffee, walks, board games) before niche activities.

### Gaps
- Sri Lanka's row in the Gallup/Meta 142-country loneliness map was not extracted (interactive data).
- Independent (non-company) usage data for Timeleft, Bumble BFF, Meetup in South Asia.

## Q8. Pain points of organising group activities: no-shows, coordination, filling team-sport numbers

### Takeaway
No SL-specific study of flaking or under-filled games was found. The best signals are a local startup's pitch (Volt: "End the WhatsApp scrum", "Never play a man short") and international event data showing free events lose about 28% of registrants (37% for small 10 to 49 person events).

### Cited Findings
- Volt (Colombo) frames the problem as coordination across multiple WhatsApp groups, double bookings, hunting for open courts and being short of players ("Never play a man short"); it does not explicitly claim to cut no-shows — [Volt](https://www.volt.lk/)
- PheedLoop analysis of 1,070+ events (Apr 2026): median no-show about 20%; free events about 28% versus paid about 17%; 22% of free events lose more than half their expected attendance; free events with 10 to 49 attendees have a median no-show of about 37% (snippet) — [PheedLoop Event Data Lab #05](https://pheedloop.com/blog/event-data-lab-report-5)
- Vendor estimates of 40 to 60% no-shows for free RSVP events are common but loosely sourced — [Ticket Fairy estimator](https://www.ticketfairy.com/tools/no-show-rate-estimator.md); [Skift Meetings, Nov 2025](https://meetings.skift.com/2025/11/26/no-shows-create-stress-wreak-havoc-on-events/); a $100 deposit experiment cut no-shows to 10% — [Skift Meetings, Sep 2026](https://meetings.skift.com/2026/09/02/the-100-experiment-that-slashed-no-show-rates-to-just-10/)
- Tabletop SL handles attendance with walk-in for first-timers and RSVP afterwards, plus a venue capacity of about 75 — [Sunday Times, 2023](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/230910/magazine/board-games-going-back-in-time-but-with-a-twist-531640.html)
- Futsal team size (5-a-side, so 10 on court plus subs, as in the brief) and Colombo venue pricing: Turfify lists hourly rates and WhatsApp contact for each of 9 Colombo venues (rates not extracted) — [Turfify](https://turfify.theatom.lk/sports/futsal/colombo)

### Inferences
- A 3 to 8 person gig is in the size band with the worst free-event no-show rates (about 37% median at 10 to 49, likely worse at small sizes). One no-show can drop a gig below Trimegos's 3-person minimum, so reliability features (reminders, reputation, waitlists) matter more than for large events.
- Splitting court cost across 10 to 12 people makes each no-show a direct cost to the rest. That is a sharper pain for futsal and badminton than for coffee.

### Gaps
- SL-specific no-show or flake rates, and how often futsal/badminton games in Colombo run short. Primary research: interview 10 to 15 regular futsal/badminton organisers in Colombo (how they fill games, average no-shows per game, how cost is split and chased, WhatsApp group sizes), and ask venue owners about cancellation rates.
- Women's specific barriers (safety, being the only woman in a group, transport after dark) when joining strangers' activities in Colombo: no data found; key interview topic given Trimegos's audience filters.
