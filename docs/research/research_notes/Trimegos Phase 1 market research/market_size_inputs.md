# Market-size inputs for Trimegos (Colombo, then Sri Lanka, then expansion markets)

All access dates are 2026-10-31 unless stated. Currency: LKR, converted at LKR 331 = USD 1. "Census 2024" means the Department of Census and Statistics (DCS) Census of Population and Housing 2024 (census moment 00:00 on 19 Dec 2024, final tables released 30 Oct 2025). Age figures marked "est." are my arithmetic on the official 5-year bands. The method is set out under each item.

## Q1. Population aged 18 to 35 (CMC area, Colombo District, Western Province, Sri Lanka, Kandy, Galle; urban and rural)

### Takeaway
The final Census 2024 tables give 5-year age bands for each district. On that base Sri Lanka has about **5.1 million people aged 18 to 34** (est.) out of 21.78 million. Colombo District has about **599k** and the Western Province about **1.48 million**. No age breakdown is published for the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) area. Its total population is not confirmed from a primary source either: the secondary figure is about 395k. Colombo District is about 75% urban, against 17.5% for the whole country.

### Cited Findings
**Headline census figures (Census 2024, final):**
- Sri Lanka total population is 21,781,800 (51.7% female). Under-15s are 20.7% (25.2% in 2012), people aged 65+ are 12.6%, and ages 15-64 are 66.7%. There are 6,111,315 households, averaging 3.5 persons. Data release date: 30 Oct 2025. ### Inferences
- CMC 18-34 proxy: applying Colombo District's 18-34 share (599,225 / 2,375,415 = 25.2%) to the unverified CMC total of 394,533 gives roughly **100k**. The weakness is that the CMC age mix probably differs from the district's, since the inner city is older and shrinking.
- A wider "Colombo core" made up of the Colombo and Thimbirigasyaya DS divisions holds 509,207 people, about **128k aged 18-34** at the district share. Adding Dehiwala, Kotte, Kolonnawa and Maharagama ("Greater Colombo inner ring") brings this to about 1.11 million people and about 280k aged 18-34.
- For the model, the Western Province 18-34 figure (about 1.48 million) is a sensible "Colombo metro" SAM base, because commuting ties Gampaha and Kalutara into Colombo.
- Kandy (about 329k) and Galle (about 249k) aged 18-34 are the obvious second-wave district pools.

### Gaps
- No official CMC-area population or age table was found. The DS boundaries (Colombo, Thimbirigasyaya) do not match the CMC boundary. The DCS GN-level file (https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/Population/CPH_2024/GN_population_pdf.pdf) gives only broad ages (<15, 15-59, 60-64, 65+). GN divisions could be summed to the CMC boundary, but I did not do that.
- I did not extract Kandy MC and Galle MC city populations.

## Q2. Internet, smartphone and social media penetration

### Takeaway
Sri Lanka had **13.9 million internet users (59.7%) in Oct 2025** on DataReportal's UN-based population. DCS's own survey puts internet use (last 12 months, ages 5-69) at 54.6% in 2024, but at **79-88% for ages 15-34** and 67.1% in the urban sector. About 80% of internet access is by smartphone. Facebook ad reach is 9.0 million and TikTok reaches 6.79 million adults.

### Cited Findings
- Internet users: 13.9 million (Oct 2025), 59.7% of population. Offline: 9.37 million (40.3%). Cellular connections: 30.3 million (130% of population, end-2025). [DataReportal Digital 2026 Sri Lanka](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka); [Daily Mirror report on it](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/front-page/40-3-per-cent-of-Sri-Lankans-still-offline-in-2025/238-331925)
- Social media user identities: 9.00 million (Oct 2025), equal to 38.7% of population and 52.5% of adults 18+. Of these, 43.4% are female. These are identities, not necessarily unique people. [DataReportal Digital 2026 Sri Lanka](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka)
- Platform ad reach, late 2025 (ad reach is not MAU):
  - Facebook: 9.00 million (52.5% of adults 18+)
  - YouTube: 8.82 million
  - TikTok: 6.79 million aged 18+ (39.7% of adults)
  - Messenger: 4.05 million
  - LinkedIn: 2.90 million registered members
  - Instagram: 2.25 million (12.6% of adults)
  - Reddit: 471k
  - X: 250k

  [DataReportal Digital 2026 Sri Lanka](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka)
- Conflict: NapoleonCat (platform ad-tool data) lists Facebook at 10,871,500 users in 2026. Its method differs from DataReportal's. [NapoleonCat Sri Lanka 2026](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/social-media-users-in-sri_lanka/2026/)
- **DCS Computer Literacy Statistics 2024 (Annual Bulletin):**
  - Internet used at least once in the last 12 months, household population aged 5-69, 2024: Sri Lanka 54.6% (48.3% in 2022, 51.2% in 2023). Urban 67.1%, rural 52.8%, estate 37.7%.
  - By age, 2024: 15-19: 79.2%; **20-24: 88.4%**; 25-29: 84.9%; 30-34: 79.6%; 35-39: 71.9%; 40-49: 55.5%.
  - Devices used to connect, 2024: smartphone 80.3%, desktop/laptop 17.0%. Urban: smartphone 74.2%, laptop 22.5%. Ages 20-24: smartphone 76.4%.
  - Computer literacy: 35.9% nationally (39.0% in 2023), 47.3% urban, 46.0% in Western Province, 70.2% for ages 20-24. "Computer literate" includes using a smartphone or tablet on one's own.

  [DCS Computer Literacy Statistics 2024 Annual Bulletin (PDF)](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/ComputerLiteracy/Bulletins/AnnualBuletinComputerLiteracy-2024.pdf)
- Digital literacy (ages 5-69) was 64.3% in the first half of 2024. [DCS 2024 first-six-months bulletin (PDF)](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/ComputerLiteracy/Bulletins/2024-FirstSixMonths.pdf)

### Inferences
- Reachable-online share of 18-34s: the DCS age rates for 2024 (about 79-88%) applied to the census 18-34 base give roughly 4.2-4.4 million young adult internet users nationally. Western Province is likely above that average, given urban 67% against a national 55% for all ages. This is a derived figure, not published.
- Smartphone-first access (80%) supports a PWA-only approach. The 20-24 cohort is where internet use peaks.
- DataReportal's rates (59.7%) are computed on 23.3 million people. On the census base of 21.78 million, 13.9 million users would be 63.8%. State which base the model uses.

### Gaps
- No smartphone *ownership* rate by age or district was found (DCS reports the device used, not ownership). The 2026 TRCSL subscriber/broadband statistics were not retrieved.
- No district-level internet-use rate was found (the bulletin gives province and sector only).

## Q3. Labour force, university students, IT/BPM workforce

### Takeaway
The labour force is 8.41 million (2025, LFPR 49.4%). Youth unemployment (15-24) is 19.5%. Public universities enrolled 147,760 internal undergraduates in 2024, plus about 202,500 external, open and distance learners. IT-BPM employment is roughly 115k-150k, but only from secondary sources.

### Cited Findings
- LFS 2025 annual:
  - Labour force 8,408,251 (male 5,449,708, female 2,958,543). LFPR 49.4%. Unemployment 3.9% (329,211 people).
  - Unemployment by age, 2025: 15-24: 153,610 (19.5%; male 16.5%, female 24.5%); 25-29: 82,274 (10.6%); 30-39: 51,923 (3.0%); 40+: 41,404 (0.8%).
  - Colombo District employment by sector: services 71.8%, industry 25.7%, agriculture 2.5%. The Western Province figures are 63.3% / 31.7% / 5.0%.

  [DCS LFS Annual Bulletin 2025 (PDF)](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/LabourForce/Bulletins/LFS_Annual%20Bulletin_2025.pdf)
- 2024 comparison: labour force 8,316 thousand, LFPR 47.4% (CBSL). [CBSL Socio-Economic Data 2025 (PDF)](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/publications/otherpub/publication_sri_lanka_socio_economic_data_folder_2025_e.pdf)
- UGC 2024 enrolment (universities and HEIs under the Universities Act, excluding backlog and the Open University in the main chart):
  - Undergraduates through the UGC intake: **147,760**. Largest groups are Arts 37,165 and Management & Commerce 29,742.
  - External, open and distance learning undergraduates: **202,502** (my sum of the column; Arts external is 116,271).
  - Foreign undergraduates: about 534.
  - Local postgraduates: 49,307.
  - The gender split in the chart is 66.05% / 33.95%. The label order is unclear from the PDF, but Sri Lankan universities are majority female.

  [UGC University Statistics 2024, Chapter 3 (PDF)](https://www.ugc.ac.lk/downloads/statistics/stat_2024/Chapter%203.pdf)
- University of Moratuwa alone had 10,918 undergraduates (incl. early-2024 intake). [UoM facts and stats](https://uom.lk/facts-and-stats-about-uom)
- IT-BPM workforce (all secondary or older):
  - About 150,000 direct and about 250,000 indirect employees (undated Sunday Observer piece, around 2023). [Sunday Observer](https://archives1.sundayobserver.lk/node/65805)
  - SLASSCOM said in 2023 that the sector adds about 10,000 jobs a year. [Echelon](https://echelon.lk/slasscom-chairman-on-unlocking-the-tech-potential/)
  - Workforce grew 22% in 2022 (SLASSCOM). [Outsource Accelerator](https://news.outsourceaccelerator.com/sri-lankas-it-bpm-up-22/)
  - An aggregator gives 140,000-144,000 in total and 115,000+ at SLASSCOM members. This is unverified. [StealthAgents](https://stealthagents.com/research/sri-lanka-bpo-industry-statistics-2026)
  - Primary baseline: ICTA National IT-BPM Workforce Survey 2019. [ICTA (PDF)](https://www.icta.lk/icta-assets/uploads/2023/02/NATIONAL-IT-BPM-WORKFORCE-SURVEY-2019-1-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- Many campuses (Colombo, Moratuwa, Sri Jayewardenepura, Kelaniya, plus private institutes such as SLIIT, NSBM and IIT) and most IT-BPM firms sit in the Western Province. Together, students and tech workers form a dense early-adopter segment, perhaps 150k-300k people in greater Colombo. This is an inference, and the two groups overlap.
- High youth unemployment (19.5%) plus a large external-degree population point to a time-rich, cash-poor segment. That favours free or low-cost gigs over paid ones.

### Gaps
- No count of **private higher-education students** was found (no recent Ministry of Education or UGC figure for non-state degree awarding institutes). As a proxy, sum enrolment disclosures from the largest institutes (SLIIT, NSBM, IIT, APIIT, CINEC). The weakness is that this is incomplete and self-reported.
- No 2024/2025 SLASSCOM or ICTA primary workforce figure was retrieved, and there is no Colombo-only IT-BPM headcount.
- I did not extract labour force by district (LFS district tables exist in the full annual report: https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/LabourForce/Annual_Reports/LFS2025.pdf).

## Q4. Venue counts (cafes/restaurants, futsal, badminton, gyms, studios, board-game cafes)

### Takeaway
No official venue census exists. The best proxy is xMap's Google-Maps-style aggregation (Mar 2025). It counts **15,547 dining and drinking locations in Colombo District**, about 4,340 of them in the Colombo + Thimbirigasyaya areas, plus 6,378 coffee/tea shops nationally. It also counts **1,855 sports and fitness locations in Colombo District** and 2,654 "gyms and studios" nationally. Directory counts of bookable futsal and badminton courts in Colombo are in single digits to low tens.

### Cited Findings
- **xMap Dining & Drinking, Sri Lanka (aggregated 19 Mar 2025):**
  - National total: 68,717 (the page header says 65,504+, an internal inconsistency), including 9,319 closed.
  - By category: restaurants & eateries 31,024; bakeries & desserts 12,305; coffee & tea shops **6,378**; F&B services 7,686.
  - By district: Colombo 15,547; Gampaha 9,561; Kandy 4,075; Kalutara 3,384; Galle 3,143.
  - By city: Colombo 2,178; Thimbirigasyaya 2,166; Kaduwela 2,152; Dehiwala-Mt Lavinia 1,709; Kesbewa 1,463; Maharagama 1,459.
  - 2,162 Colombo listings are flagged closed.

  The method is aggregated public sources, partner APIs and crawlers. [xMap Dining & Drinking Sri Lanka](https://www.xmap.ai/location-intelligence-reports/dining--drinking-locations-in-sri-lanka)
- **xMap Sports & Fitness, Sri Lanka (19 Mar 2025):**
  - Total: 7,833 (the body text also says 8,388).
  - By category: gyms & studios **2,654**; ball sports 1,496; recreation centres 553; martial arts 220; team sports & fields 175; personal training 151.
  - By area: Colombo District 1,855; Gampaha 1,049; Kandy 503; Galle 445; Thimbirigasyaya 355; Colombo city 230.

  [xMap Sports & Fitness Sri Lanka](https://www.xmap.ai/location-intelligence-reports/sports--fitness-locations-in-sri-lanka)
- Sri Lanka Tourism's registered-restaurant directory lists 842 entries over 106 pages, some with expired licences. Tourist-grade registration is mandatory for tourism businesses, so this is a subset of all restaurants. [Sri Lanka Tourism restaurants directory](https://www.srilanka.travel/restaurants)
- PickMe says it gives access to "over 4,000 merchants" of all types (restaurants, stores, cloud kitchens, home bakers). An older PickMe FAQ said "over 1000". [PickMe merchants](https://pickme.lk/merchant/); [PickMe FAQ](https://pickme.lk/faqs/)
- Uber Eats: no national partner count. Gampaha was its 5th Sri Lankan city at launch, with 30+ restaurants (2020-era). [Uber newsroom](https://www.uber.com/en-LK/newsroom/uber-eats-launches-in-gampaha-its-5th-city-in-sri-lanka)
- Gyms in Colombo: a business-list vendor counts 143. [Rentech Digital](https://rentechdigital.com/smartscraper/business-report-details/sri-lanka/western-province/list-of-gyms-in-colombo) (A search snippet said the Power World chain runs 21 fitness centres, but I could not tie this to a specific page, so it is unverified and should not be used.)
- Futsal: Turfify lists 9 futsal venues in Colombo (including Kolonnawa). [Turfify Colombo futsal](https://turfify.theatom.lk/sports/futsal/colombo)
- Playo lists **5** bookable venues in Colombo: CR7 Futsal & Indoor Cricket (Col 3), MBA Badminton Courts (Col 7), Uni Sports Center Alfred House (Col 3), X Sports Arena (Col 15), Shuttle Power Sports Complex (Col 6). [Playo Colombo venues](https://playo.co/venues/colombo/sports/all)
- Bookin360 is another local court-booking directory. [Bookin360](https://bookin360.com/)

### Inferences
- Venue-side TAM for paid group-visit subscriptions, at the placeholder fees of **LKR 5,000 (USD 15.1)** and **LKR 15,000 (USD 45.3)** per month:
  - Colombo District has about 15.5k F&B locations, of which roughly 6-7k cafes/eateries are realistic targets (inference).
  - Colombo District has about 1.9k sports and fitness locations.
  - So an upper-bound venue universe is on the order of 10k-17k venues in Colombo District. The realistically addressable set of youth-oriented, group-friendly, bookable venues is probably 1-2 orders of magnitude smaller, in the hundreds. This is an inference to validate by sampling.
- Futsal and badminton venues that take online bookings in Colombo number about 10-30 (directories overlap). That pool is small enough to enumerate by hand.

### Gaps
- No count was found for yoga/dance studios, board-game cafes or badminton courts. Proxy: a manual Google Maps or OpenStreetMap category count inside the CMC and Western Province boundaries. The weakness is duplicates, closures and miscategorised listings (xMap already flags 9,319 closed dining listings).
- xMap's definitions are opaque and its totals are internally inconsistent (65,504 vs 68,717; 7,833 vs 8,388).
- No Uber Eats or PickMe Food restaurant-only counts by city.

## Q5. Spending: income, expenditure, recreation and eating out

### Takeaway
The latest published HIES is **2019**, which predates the 2022 crisis and its inflation. It gives Colombo District mean household expenditure of LKR 108,893 a month (USD 329 at today's rate; median LKR 81,082). The published summary has no recreation or eating-out line items.

### Cited Findings
- HIES 2019, Sri Lanka: mean household income LKR 76,414 a month (median 53,333). Mean household expenditure LKR 63,130 (median 47,544), up 14.8% from 2016. [DCS HIES 2019 Final Results (PDF)](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/IncomeAndExpenditure/HouseholdIncomeandExpenditureSurvey2019FinalResults.pdf)
- HIES 2019 by area (mean / median household income; mean / median expenditure, LKR per month) [DCS HIES 2019 (PDF)](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/IncomeAndExpenditure/HouseholdIncomeandExpenditureSurvey2019FinalResults.pdf):
  - **Colombo District:** income 132,433 / 86,981; expenditure **108,893 / 81,082**
  - Western Province: income 109,813 / 75,000; expenditure 90,243 / 68,290
  - Urban sector: income 116,670 / 74,679; expenditure 95,392 / 69,300
  - Gampaha: expenditure 84,413 / 65,037
  - Kalutara: expenditure 65,970 / 54,828
  - Kandy: expenditure 66,997 / 48,432
  - Galle: expenditure 58,504 / 45,628
  - Top-quintile mean income nationally: 196,289 (51.4% of all income)
- The HIES collects 35 sub-categories (19 food, 16 non-food), but the summary PDF does not break out "food taken outside" or recreation. [DCS HIES 2019 (PDF)](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/IncomeAndExpenditure/HouseholdIncomeandExpenditureSurvey2019FinalResults.pdf)
- A CBSL data folder shows a "cultural activities and entertainment" spending share of roughly 0.9-1.9% across quintiles. The year and amount are unconfirmed (search snippet only). [CBSL Socio-Economic Data 2025 (PDF)](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/publications/otherpub/publication_sri_lanka_socio_economic_data_folder_2025_e.pdf)

### Inferences
- 2019 rupee figures need inflating to 2026. Consumer prices roughly doubled over 2021-2023, so nominal 2019 figures understate current spend substantially. The model should apply the CCPI index (not retrieved here) rather than use the 2019 LKR figures directly.
- If recreation/culture is around 1-2% of expenditure, a Colombo household spends roughly LKR 1-2k a month on it at 2019 prices. This is an order-of-magnitude inference from an unconfirmed share.

### Gaps
- No HIES newer than 2019 was found (check whether HIES 2024/25 results exist).
- No eating-out or recreation line-item amounts, and no average restaurant or cafe ticket size in Colombo. Proxy: sample menu prices on PickMe Food or Uber Eats, or venue interviews. The weakness is that delivery prices carry a mark-up and are not dine-in tickets.

## Q6. Expansion markets: diaspora and comparable South Asian cities, with competitor presence

### Takeaway
The Sri Lankan emigrant stock is about 1.49 million (UN DESA 2024). The largest settled communities are in the UK (about 147k Sri Lanka-born), Australia (172.8k in 2024) and Canada (about 136k born). The Gulf populations are large but temporary workers. India is already served by Playo, Hudle and Timeleft (in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Hyderabad).

### Cited Findings
**Sri Lankan diaspora**
- Global: about 1.49 million emigrants (UN DESA, 2024), via a Wikipedia summary. [Wikipedia: Sri Lankan diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_diaspora)
- Australia: 172,800 Sri Lanka-born residents at end-June 2024 (primary source). [Australian Dept of Home Affairs country profile](https://www.homeaffairs.gov.au/research-and-statistics/statistics/country-profiles/profiles/sri-lanka) Conflict: the 2021 Census gives 153,267 born, or 131,905 by ancestry, per Wikipedia. [Wikipedia: Sri Lankan Australians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Australians)
- UK: 147,195 Sri Lanka-born (2021/22 censuses) and 149,239 of Sri Lankan ethnicity (England and Wales, 2021), via Wikipedia. Descent-based estimates run up to about 400k. [Wikipedia: British Sri Lankans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Sri_Lankans)
- Canada: 136,240 Sri Lanka-born (2021 census), via Wikipedia. Older broad estimates of about 450k included descendants. [Wikipedia: Sri Lankan Canadians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Canadians)
- Italy: 108,069 (2022). UAE: 320,000 (2023). Qatar: 145,256 (2016). Kuwait: 99,858 (2016). All via the Wikipedia compilation. [Wikipedia: Sri Lankan diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_diaspora)

**Comparable cities**
- Dhaka urban agglomeration: 36.6 million (UN WUP 2025, new delineation method). The UN press release says "almost 40 million". [UN DESA WUP 2025 press release (PDF)](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/sites/www.un.org.development.desa.pd/files/undesa_pd_2025_press_release_wup25.pdf); [Visual Capitalist ranking](https://www.visualcapitalist.com/ranked-the-worlds-30-largest-cities-by-population/)

### Inferences
- The diaspora is a weak market for in-person gigs, which need local density. ## Q7. Penetration benchmarks for calibrating SOM (Meetup, Timeleft, Bumble BFF, Playo)

### Takeaway
No competitor publishes users by city as a share of young adults. The best available signals are global totals:
- Timeleft: about 150k diners a month across about 220 cities, €18M ARR.
- Meetup: 60 million registered, with Gen Z and young Millennials at 40% of MAU.
- Playo: 5 million+ users.

Any per-city penetration rate has to be derived, and it will be crude.

### Cited Findings
- Timeleft: about 150,000 diners a month across 200+ cities, about 100 staff and €18M ARR after 20 months. It peaked at 325 cities and cut back to about 220. [Tim Frin Substack profile](https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-timelefts-journey-to-connecting)
- Conflict: Timeleft claims 4M+ members in 200+ cities in 52 countries, while its Google Play listing says 3M+ users, 160+ cities, 48 countries. [Timeleft blog](https://timeleft.com/blog/best-apps-to-make-friends/); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.timeleft.app&hl=en)
- Meetup: 60 million+ registered users (2023 report). Adult Gen Z and young Millennials make up 40% of MAU (Sep 2025), with no MAU total published. [Meetup 2023 Measurement Report (PDF)](https://www.meetup.com/marketing-assets/PDF/Meetup+Trend+Report+v13.pdf); [Business Wire, Sep 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250930663024/en/Meetup-Unveils-Fresh-New-Design-as-Gen-Z-and-Millennials-Crave-More-In-Person-Interactions)
- Bumble: about 35 million active users and 2.4 million paying (2025, Business of Apps). BFF claims 3 million registered users (EarthWeb, low quality). BFF was split into a standalone app in the US in 2025. [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/bumble-statistics/); [EarthWeb](https://earthweb.com/how-many-people-use-bumble/); [Wikipedia: Bumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumble)
- Playo: 5M+ users and 5,000+ venues (self-reported). Hudle: 1.5M players. [Playo blog](https://blog.playo.co/best-sports-booking-apps-india/); [StartupPoint](https://startuppoint.in/beyond-the-game-how-indias-everyday-athlete/)

### Inferences
- Timeleft's 150k monthly diners across about 220 cities averages about 680 paying participants per city per month. That is a sanity anchor for how few people a mature "meet strangers" product moves in a typical city. Large capitals will be far above the mean and small cities below it.
- An illustrative SOM check (not a forecast):
  - 0.5-2% of Colombo District's 18-34s (599k) would be 3k-12k monthly participants.
  - The same share of Western Province 18-34s (1.48M) would be 7k-30k.
  - Timeleft's per-city average suggests the low end is realistic for the first years.
- Playo (5M users across about 110-150 cities, with India's urban youth in the hundreds of millions) implies low single-digit penetration even for the category leader. This is an inference without a denominator.

### Gaps
- No city-level user counts were found for Meetup, Timeleft, Bumble BFF or Playo in any city, and no survey of the share of young adults using such apps. Proxies:
  1. Meetup group member counts for a city, scraped from meetup.com/find. The weakness is that this counts members, not active users, and there is heavy double counting.
  2. App-intelligence estimates (Sensor Tower, data.ai) by country. These are paywalled.
- No Bumble filing gives a BFF-only MAU figure.
